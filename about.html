🌟 New Ownership · June 2025

A Community Tradition,
Renewed

Kidz Kaboodle has been serving Tacoma families since the early 2000s. In June 2025, the store passed into new hands — a local family committed to carrying on everything that has made Kidz Kaboodle a Tacoma staple for over 20 years.

We're a small, family-run business. We're here every day, we know our regulars, and we genuinely care about providing great value — for the families who shop with us and the ones who trade with us.

Our mission is simple: give kids' gear a second life, help families stretch their budgets, and keep great stuff out of the landfill. We're proud to be part of that cycle in our community.

What We're About

Three things we care about, every day.

💰

Real Value

Kids grow fast and gear adds up. We keep prices honest so families can afford what they need — and earn something back when their kids move on to the next size.

♻️

Reuse & Sustainability

Every item that gets a second home is one less thing in a landfill. We think that's worth celebrating. Resale isn't just practical — it's good for the planet.

🏘️

Community Roots

We're a Tacoma shop, through and through. Our customers are our neighbors. We've been trusted by this community for over 20 years, and we take that seriously.
