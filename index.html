Have kids' stuff to sell?

We buy gently-used kids' items Mon, Tue & Thu · 11am–2pm (check in by 1:30pm for a same-day offer). Cash or store credit, paid on the spot — no consignment, no waiting.

LEARN HOW IT WORKS →
1
📦 Bring It In

One store-provided 18-gallon bin of clean, gently-used kids items. Check in by 1:30pm for a same-day offer or equipment trade (Mon, Tue & Thu only). Equipment must be fully assembled outside before we take a look.

2
🔍 We Review

Wait in store (15–45 min) or we can call you within a week with an offer. Any customer can request approval before accepting.

3
💸 Get Paid

Earn a percentage of our resale price — paid on the spot. Choose store credit and get even more value for your items.

4
🎁 Donate or Take It Home

Items we pass on can go home with you or be donated to the store. Most donations end up on our 25¢ table or our monthly free giveaways!
