✅ Condition We Look For

  • ✔ Clean and freshly laundered clothing
  • ✔ No stains, rips, holes, or heavy pilling
  • ✔ All parts and accessories included (for gear)
  • ✔ Working electronics and battery-operated items
  • ✔ Car seats within their expiration date
  • ✔ Items free of pet hair and smoke odors

❌ What We Pass On

  • ✗ Stained, torn, or heavily worn items
  • ✗ Items missing parts or accessories
  • ✗ Recalled products (we check the CPSC list)
  • ✗ Expired car seats or safety restraints
  • ✗ Mattresses and sleep surfaces
  • ✗ Items with strong odors (smoke, mildew)

Tips for Getting Items Accepted

A little prep goes a long way — here's how to set yourself up for success.

🧺

Come In Clean

Wash everything before you bring it in. Freshly laundered items make a much better impression and are more likely to be accepted.

📂

Group by Size

Sort your clothing by size before you arrive — all 3T together, all 4T together, etc. It speeds up our review and shows you care about your items.

🌞

Bring Seasonal Items

We accept more of what's seasonally relevant. Summer items in summer, winter coats in fall. Off-season items are harder to place.

🏅

Better Brands, Better Offers

Gap, Carter's, Nike, Patagonia, North Face, Columbia — recognizable brands command better offers than unbranded generics.

🔩

Bring All the Parts

Gear and toys with all parts, straps, accessories, and batteries included are far more likely to be accepted at a better price.

🐾

Watch for Odors

Items that smell like smoke or pet odors are automatically passed on. Give things a fresh wash and a sniff test before you pack them up.

What Happens If We Pass on an Item?

Everything we don't take gets handed right back to you — no questions asked. We won't keep your items or donate them without your permission.

We recommend bringing a bag or tote to carry home anything we pass on. If you'd rather donate the items instead of taking them back, just let us know and we can point you to a local resource.

Not every item is a match every time — stock needs change weekly. Something we pass on today might be something we want in a few weeks. Follow our Facebook or Instagram to stay current on what we're actively looking for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what we hear most often.

❓ Do I need an appointment?

Nope! Just walk in during trade hours — Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 10am–2pm. No appointment, no scheduling, no waiting list. First come, first served.

❓ How long does the process take?

Usually 15–30 minutes, depending on how many items you bring. If you arrive with a large load, it may take a bit longer. We recommend arriving by 1:30pm to leave enough time before we close trade drop-offs at 2pm.

❓ Do you take expired car seats?

No — safety is our top priority. We only accept car seats and booster seats that are within their manufacturer expiration date. The expiration date is stamped directly on the seat. We check every one.

❓ Can I trade online or ship items to you?

We're an in-store trade only — bring your items in person during trade hours. This lets our team evaluate each item hands-on, which is how we keep quality high and offer you fair prices.
