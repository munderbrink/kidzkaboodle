How It Works

Same-day, straightforward, no strings attached.

1

Tips for Getting Items Accepted

A little prep goes a long way.

🧺

Come In Clean

Wash everything before you bring it in. Freshly laundered items are far more likely to be accepted.

📂

Lay Flat & Unfolded

Lay clothing items flat and unfolded — it helps us process more efficiently and speeds up your offer.

🌞

Stay Seasonal

We buy what's in season. We don't purchase out-of-season clothing — check our Facebook for what's currently in demand.

🏅

Better Brands, Better Offers

Gap, Carter's, Nike, Patagonia, North Face — recognizable brands get better offers than unbranded generics.

🔩

Bring All the Parts

Gear and toys must be complete to be considered. Bring all parts, straps, and accessories — and include batteries or chargers for anything that requires power. A toy that doesn't turn on gets passed on.

🐾

Watch for Odors

Items that smell like smoke or pets are automatically passed on. Give things a fresh wash and a sniff test before packing up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what we hear most often.

❓ Do I need an appointment?

Nope! Drop-offs are welcome any trade day — Mon, Tue & Thu 11am–2pm and Sundays 11am–1pm. For a same-day offer or equipment trade (Mon/Tue/Thu only), check in by 1:30pm and plan to stay while we review.

❓ What is the 1:30pm cutoff?

To receive a same-day offer — including all equipment trades — you must be checked in by 1:30pm on Mon, Tue, and Thu. No exceptions. If you arrive after 1:30pm, we won't be able to process your items that day.

❓ Are trade hours always the same?

We do our best to maintain consistent trade hours, but we do occasionally close for holidays, staffing, or scheduled store rotation and refresh weeks. We post all closures promptly on our Facebook page — check there before making a trip if you're unsure.

❓ How long does the process take?

Usually 15–45 minutes depending on how many items you bring. If you'd rather not wait, you can request approval and we'll contact you within the week with an offer.

❓ Do you take expired car seats?

No — safety is our top priority. We only accept car seats within their manufacturer expiration date. The expiration is stamped directly on the seat. We check every one. Car seats must have at least 2 years remaining before expiration.

❓ Can I trade online or ship items?

In-store only — bring your items in person during trade hours. This lets our team evaluate each item hands-on, which is how we keep quality high and offer fair prices.
