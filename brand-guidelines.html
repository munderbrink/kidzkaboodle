07 — Voice & Tone

✅ Do

  • ✔ Be specific — give the actual hours, actual cutoffs
  • ✔ Be direct — skip "We'd be happy to…"
  • ✔ Warm and community-first
  • ✔ Honest about what we pass on (not apologetic)
  • ✔ Use Facebook as the live source of truth for current info
  • ✔ Acknowledge neighbors, families, sustainability

❌ Don't

  • ✗ "Tacoma's favorite" — other good shops exist
  • ✗ Corporate or stiff phrasing
  • ✗ Overly promotional / hard sell
  • ✗ Internal process details on public pages
  • ✗ Payout % anywhere except the Sell/Trade page
  • ✗ Sign off with a person's name ("Thanks, Kristi")

Example — trade hours: "We buy gently-used kids' items Mon, Tue & Thu · 11am–2pm (check in by 1:30pm for a same-day offer). Cash or store credit, paid on the spot — no consignment, no waiting."

08 — Standing Design Rules
📘 Facebook is live source of truth — always direct customers there for current inventory, what we're buying, and trade closures. Every page should have a visible Facebook CTA above the fold.
💰 Payout percentages on Sell/Trade page only — homepage and other pages use "earn a percentage of our resale price." Specific rates (30%/50% equipment, 20%/40% clothing) live only on trade.html.
♻️ Community + sustainability angle — the About page establishes new ownership (June 2025), family-run business, 20+ year tradition. Don't overclaim or oversell heritage.
⏰ Always include the 1:30pm hard cutoff in any trade hours reference, not just the "11am–2pm" window. Drop-offs are available Mon/Tue/Thu and Sunday — clarify same-day vs. drop-off whenever trade hours are mentioned.
📸 Team photo placeholder is live on about.html — swap when the photo is ready. Reference the existing img tag format used there.
